The Process

From blank page to bound novel in four steps

01

Write Your Story

Submit your complete manuscript — your characters, plot, dialogue, and settings. Be thorough. The more detail you give us, the richer your graphic novel will be. We require long-form writing (8,000+ words). This is not a short-story platform.

02

QUILL AI Illustrates

Our proprietary QUILL engine reads your prose and intelligently panels it — composing scenes, rendering characters, setting mood and atmosphere — producing 50 to 100 illustrated pages faithful to your vision.

03

Review in Your Workspace

Your illustrated draft is sent directly to your personal workspace. Review every panel, request revisions, and approve the final layout before we move to print or digital distribution.

04

We Publish & Deliver

Choose to keep it private, share it digitally, or let us publish it. We handle formatting, distribution, and delivery — a finished graphic novel shipped right to your door or released online.
